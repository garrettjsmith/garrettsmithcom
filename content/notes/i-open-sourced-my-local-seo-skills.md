What if you woke up every morning and your local SEO tasks were already done.

Not templates you have to fill in. Done. Written. Waiting in a folder.

Rankings checked. Review velocity tracked. GBP post drafts. Competitor movements noted. All of it ready for you to actually send to a client.

## **That's how I’m working now.**

I’ve been doing SEO professionally for 25 years. 15 in Local SEO.

I've also been building with AI for the past 3 years. I started with ChatGPT in January 2023 writing scripts that turned into 20 tools in 90 days. The past 12 months I’ve been working almost entirely inside Claude.

At some point last year I asked myself: why isn’t everyone else here doing the same thing? What if I could encode everything I know about local SEO into a system Claude could use?

Not generic prompts. Not "pretend you're an SEO expert."

The actual methodology — the frameworks, the decision trees, the tool preferences, the audit structures we’ve used with single shingles and Fortune 500 clients throughout my career.

Then folks here could leverage it to do more better work.

So I turned my knowledge and AI chat history into an operational layer for local SEO practitioners. And  I'm giving it away for FREE!

## **Introducing Local SEO Skills**

It's the answer to "what would Garrett do here?" — without Garrett in the room.

Skills are Markdown (.md) files that AI LLMs like Claude use to enhance core knowledge and operations at a user level.

They work with Claude Browser, Desktop, CLI, and any other skills based LLM you want to use.

They’re free. I open sourced them. Which means you can customize them however you want, whenever you want!

You can learn more and get started below

- GitHub: [github.com/garrettjsmith/localseoskills](https://github.com/garrettjsmith/localseoskills)
- Docs: [localseoskills.com](https://localseoskills.com)

Download. Fork. Star. 🙏

## **What Local SEO Skills actually does:**

You install it once. Then you just... talk to Claude about local SEO.

- "Audit this business."
- "Why aren't they showing up in the map pack?"
- "Draft a response to this negative review."
- "Build me a proposal for a 25-location franchise."
- "What changed since last month?"

Claude knows what to do. It uses current methodology. It pulls the right data. It gives you the output.

## **Local SEO Skills goes further than that.**

You can set up monitoring that runs automatically — rankings, reviews, GBP changes, competitor movements. Weekly. Daily. Whatever you need.

You can run on Anthropic's servers, not your computer. You get notified when something matters.

You can set up briefs for each client that accumulate history over time. After a few months, Claude knows that client the way you do. Context compounds.

You can queue up GBP posts and review responses for your approval — drafted automatically, executed when you say go.

Heck, if you give Claude email access you could have it send the approval email too.

## **This is what AI-native local SEO looks like.**

Not asking ChatGPT for blog post ideas. Not seeing Claude can generate a list of keywords. Not using AI as a fancy autocomplete.

AI as the operating layer. You bring the expertise and the client relationships.

The system handles the execution, the monitoring, the reporting, the drafting.

## **What's inside Local SEO Skills:**

- **24 strategy skills** — Geogrid rankings, GBP, audits, citations, reviews, keywords, competitor analysis, AI visibility, content, reporting
- **12 tool integrations** — connects to [Local SEO Data](https://localseodata.com), Local Falcon, [LSA Spy](https://lsaspy.com), Semrush, Ahrefs, and more
- **15 scheduled tasks** — monitoring and reporting that runs automatically
- **Persistent briefs** — per-client memory that builds over time
- **Full documentation** — how local search works, glossary, tool routing

The best part? As SEO changes, as AI advances, so too will the Local SEO Skills. The more contributors to the repo, combined with frequent new releases, leads to better

I built it for my team. So they could operate at my level without me being the bottleneck.
