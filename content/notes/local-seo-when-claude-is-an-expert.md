Hi —

I've mentioned [Local SEO Skills](https://localseoskills.com) and [Local SEO Data](https://localseodata.com) in passing the last few weeks. Today I want to actually show you what this looks like in practice.

Because "AI-native local SEO" is a phrase that means nothing until you see it.

## **The short version**

**Local SEO Skills** is a set of 38 skills that give Claude the methodology — how to think about local SEO problems, which audits to run, what order to check things, what actually matters for a given situation.

**Local SEO Data** is an MCP and 41 API endpoints that give Claude the data — live rankings, GBP profiles, reviews, citations, AI visibility scores, geogrid scans, competitor analysis.

Skills is the brain. Data is the senses.

Together, Claude stops giving you generic advice like "make sure your GBP is complete" and starts giving you specific answers like "you're missing Emergency HVAC Service as a secondary category — all three of your top competitors have it."

## **What it actually looks like**

Here's how I run a GBP audit now:

I type: "_Audit the GBP profile for Smith's Plumbing in Phoenix and tell me what to fix_."

Claude:  
→ Routes to the right skill (gbp-optimization)  
→ Pulls the live profile data  
→ Compares against competitors in the same market  
→ Returns a prioritized list of fixes with severity scores

30-60 seconds. No dashboard hopping. No copy-pasting between tools.

Here's how I check AI visibility:

"_Check Smith's Plumbing AI visibility for 'who is an experienced plumber in Phoenix that can handle an emergency fix of a leaking water line at my house today' across all platforms._"

Claude:  
→ Queries ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity  
→ Checks if the business gets mentioned, and where  
→ Compares against competitors  
→ Returns a platform-by-platform breakdown

This used to require four browser tabs and manual queries. Now it's one sentence.

## **The difference from using Claude without this**

Claude without Local SEO Skills can tell you general local SEO advice. It knows what GBP categories are. It knows reviews matter. It can explain what citations are.

But it can't tell you which categories you're missing based on what your actual competitors have. It can't tell you your review velocity is declining while your top competitor's is accelerating. It can't tell you that you're showing up in Gemini but not ChatGPT for your primary keyword.

The skill + data layer makes the advice specific. Specific to your business, your market, your competitors, right now.

That specificity is the difference between "here are some local SEO tips" and "here's exactly what's broken and what to fix first."

## **Why I built this**

Local SEO is a workflow problem, not a data problem.

The data has always been out there. Rankings, reviews, citations, profiles — dozens of tools can show you this stuff.

What was missing was a system that actually thinks about local SEO the way an experienced practitioner does. That remembers what happened last month. That knows which audit to run first. That compounds history across client engagements.

That's what Local SEO Skills is. And Local SEO Data is the sensory layer that makes it see your actual market instead of talking in generalities.

I'm using this for everything now. Audits, proposals, reports, review responses, competitive analysis, AI visibility tracking. It's how I work.
