## **Gemini + GBP is live**

![Gemini chat with the Google Business Profile extension](https://beehiiv-images-production.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/asset/file/032d3ed8-254f-4f48-b0ea-bb84f8c122f5/image.png?t=1783965225)
<!-- image: screenshot; Gemini chat where the user types "@Google Business Profile" and the assistant replies "Hey there! I can help you manage your Google Business Profile directly from here." It lists four options: Check Performance (search metrics, keyword impressions, customer views), Manage Reviews (look at recent reviews and draft a reply), Update Information (change hours, website, food menus and services), and Share Updates (create a post or upload photos), then suggests asking to "list my businesses". -->

Google announced you can now connect your Google Business Profile directly to the Gemini app in early June.

One tap. Profile linked. Gemini now has access to your reviews, Q&A, performance data, and profile details.

**What that means:** you can ask Gemini "how did my business do this month?" and get actual analysis of your search impressions, direction requests, and call data. You can say "help me respond to my latest review" and get a draft in your brand voice. You can tell it to update your hours, post a seasonal update, or flag gaps in your profile.

It’s pretty neat. [It’s just not perfect yet](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/garrettsmith_i-was-playing-around-with-the-new-gemini-share-7482430149225771008-LMf1/?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAAABfBYIBj_X3III8Mv278Qh5XTcq7Xg7-Xo).

The Business Notebooks feature keeps your chats, sources, and profile data in one place. It surfaces alerts — unanswered questions, holiday hours not set — and gives recommendations based on your local market.

**The practical implication:** GBP management is moving from dashboard to conversation.

**The caveats:** rolling out globally except EEA/UK. Only works with single-profile accounts for now. Multi-location agencies are still waiting.

## **The Collected Info tab**

![Collected info tab in the Business information panel](https://beehiiv-images-production.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/asset/file/8899789f-bac3-4a1e-940f-b8a4dd7a2bbd/image.png?t=1783965309)

Google added a new "Collected Info" section to the Business Details section. It shows you what information Google has gathered about your business — from automated calls, texts, WhatsApp messages, and web scraping.

You can see:

1. What data was collected
2. When it was collected
3. Where it came from

And critically: you can reject it if it's wrong.

This matters because Google is actively gathering data to fill gaps in your profile. They're calling your verified phone number. They're pulling info from your website. They're crowdsourcing from users. 

The Collected Info tab is Google telling you what they're doing — and giving you a way to say "no, that's not accurate."

It's in select regions and categories for now. But the direction is clear: Google will fill in what you don't, but at least you can see what they're pulling.

Google finally shipped native post scheduling — late 2025 rollout, now fully available. You can schedule posts for a specific date and time directly from the GBP dashboard. No third-party tools required.

And for multi-location businesses: you can now compose one post and push it to all locations simultaneously.

**The tactical shift:** you can sit down Monday morning, create your posts for the week, schedule them, move on. You use to need software for this type of mass scheduling.

Recurring posts are still in testing and rolling out through 2026.

## **Reinstatement form update**

![Google Business Profile appeal form with evidence upload](https://beehiiv-images-production.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/asset/file/57204615-f517-445d-b23b-52489877c02c/image.png?t=1783965618)

This one flew under the radar, but last week Google streamlined the reinstatement appeal process.

**The old flow:** submit your appeal, then open a separate evidence form, then upload documents across five different fields within a 60-minute window. Miss the window or fumble the fields and your evidence didn't attach.

**The new flow:** evidence uploads are integrated directly into the appeal form. One pass. No separate form. No ticking clock.

Same documents accepted, same review process, just less friction. For anyone who's ever fumbled a reinstatement because the evidence form timed out — this matters.

## **ChatGPT ads are live and scaling**

![ChatGPT sponsored ad card](https://beehiiv-images-production.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/asset/file/7a68f3e8-339f-4d30-a8dc-28fde83ca091/image.png?t=1783965844)

Below it is the "Ask anything" input box and the note "ChatGPT is AI and can make mistakes." -->

OpenAI launched self-serve Ads Manager in May with no minimum spend.

The numbers:

- Free and Go tier users see ads (Plus/Pro/Team/Enterprise don't)
- CPMs dropped from $60 at launch to ~$25 now
- CPC bidding available with $3-5 bid floors by category
- Live in US, Canada, Japan, Australia, UK
- 49% of US responses now include an ad

**The Ad Format:** a sponsored card below the AI response. Title, description, image, link. Clearly labeled. Contextual — matched to the conversation topic, not the keyword.

This is the first major AI search ad platform. Look for more Ads in AI responses from Google too. But for now, if you're buying ads for local services: ChatGPT is officially a channel to consider.

## **Search Console now shows AI impressions**

![Search Console Generative AI features performance report](https://beehiiv-images-production.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/asset/file/d096e373-6cab-4d86-b6b2-78db042c4c2a/image.png?t=1783965880)

The left nav shows Generative AI under both Search results and Discover. -->

Google added dedicated AI Performance reports to Search Console in June.

For the first time, you can see:  
→ Impressions from AI Overviews  
→ Impressions from AI Mode  
→ Impressions from AI features in Discover  
→ Breakdowns by page, country, device, date

**What you can't see yet:** clicks. The reports are impressions only. Google confirmed AI impressions were always included in your overall Performance totals — this just breaks them out.

**There's also a new toggle:** you can opt out of appearing in AI Overviews, AI Mode, and Discover AI. If you opt out, you don't get traffic or impressions from those surfaces. But — and this is the important part — it doesn't affect your normal organic rankings. The opt-out is for AI features only.

Rolling out to UK first (CMA mandate), global rollout coming.

Microsoft launched AI Performance in Bing Webmaster Tools back in February. Then expanded it in June with Intents, Topics, Citation Share, and Compare.

This shows you how often your content is cited in Copilot responses. Not impressions — actual citations. Which pages get referenced, what grounding queries trigger them, how your citation share changes over time.

Google's June 3 update gives you AI impressions. Bing gives you citation data. That's a meaningful difference if you're trying to understand AI visibility.

**The practical move:** verify your site in Bing Webmaster Tools if you haven't. The AI Performance report is free and it's showing data Google isn't. Plus Bing and OpenAI are friends…

## **The zero-click numbers keep climbing**

![Stacked bar chart of searches with zero clicks vs 1+ clicks, 2016 to 2026](https://beehiiv-images-production.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/asset/file/b85819be-d11f-4403-a7f5-76f0ef848da4/image.png?t=1783966044)

Latest data from SparkToro/Similarweb (Q1 2026):

- 68% of Google searches end without a click
- Up from 60.45% in 2024
- AI Overviews trigger 83% zero-click
- AI Mode triggers 93% zero-click

AI Overviews now appear on 48% of all queries — up 58% year over year. AI Mode crossed 1 billion monthly users. AI Overviews hit 2.5 billion.

But here's the conversion flip: AI-referred traffic now converts 42% better than non-AI traffic. A year ago it converted 38% worse. The clicks are fewer, but the clicks that happen are higher intent.

**The takeaway for local:** the "near me" searcher who calls after reading an AI Overview is further down the funnel than the one who was just browsing options. Fewer clicks, may bring better clicks.

## **What to do with all this**

1. Connect your GBP to Gemini if you're a single-location business. Play with it. See what it can see about your profile and what recommendations it surfaces.
2. Check the Collected Info tab. See what Google has gathered. Reject anything wrong.
3. Schedule your posts. The excuse of "posting is too manual" is gone.
4. Get your reinstatement docs in order before you need them. The new flow is smoother but the evidence requirements haven't changed.
5. Verify your site in Bing Webmaster Tools. Pull your AI Performance data. See what's getting cited in Copilot.
6. Check your Search Console for the new AI Performance report when it rolls out to you. Baseline your AI impressions.
7. And if you're buying ads: ChatGPT is a real channel now. At least worth testing.
